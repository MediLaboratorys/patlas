# patlas
P.A.T.L.A.S. — Biomedical Research &amp; Medical Technology

P.A.T.L.A.S. Research is an independent conceptual research initiative focused on exploring the intersection of biomedical engineering, medical imaging, healthcare innovation, and emerging medical technologies. Through interdisciplinary thinking, scientific curiosity, and technology-driven exploration, P.A.T.L.A.S. Research aims to investigate new approaches to medical visualization, computational healthcare systems, oncology-related technological concepts, and next-generation diagnostic support technologies. Our projects emphasize research, conceptual design, engineering principles, and the exploration of future possibilities in modern medicine. We are committed to documenting ideas, examining scientific foundations, and encouraging responsible innovation at the intersection of medicine, engineering, and technology. All projects are currently conceptual research initiatives and do not represent clinically validated medical devices, established diagnostic systems, or approved treatments.
